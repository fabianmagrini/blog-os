# DISC and DISC-derived personality frameworks

## What it is

DISC categorizes behavior into four types — Dominance, Influence,
Steadiness, Conscientiousness — traced to William Moulton Marston's 1928
book *Emotions of Normal People*. Popular derivatives include Insights
Discovery and Thomas Erikson's *Surrounded by Idiots* (its Red/Yellow/
Green/Blue color model maps directly onto D/I/S/C).

## Reliability vs. validity — the precise, defensible critique

- **Reliability (consistency) is decent**: test-retest coefficients of
  .71–.89 reported across gaps from one week to one year.
- **Validity (does it predict anything real) is not demonstrated**:
  "psychologists question its predictive validity" and it lacks criterion
  validity against actual job/team performance outcomes.
- A study in the *Scandinavian Journal of Psychology* is reported (cited at
  one remove via Wikipedia — primary paper not independently verified) to
  have found DISC's dimensions reducible to combinations of the
  already-validated Big Five traits, suggesting DISC may repackage known
  constructs rather than add anything novel.

**Useful framing for future articles**: "not scientifically validated" is
accurate and defensible; "totally arbitrary" or "made up" overstates the
critique and is easier for a skeptical reader to knock down by pointing to
the reliability data.

## Documented direct criticism

*Surrounded by Idiots* specifically: Mattias Lundberg (associate professor
of psychology, Umeå University) publicly called its basis problematic;
author Thomas Erikson received the "Fraudster of the Year" award from
Föreningen Vetenskap och Folkbildning (Swedish Association for Science and
Public Education) in 2018 over pseudoscientific claims. (Sourced via the
book's Wikipedia entry — not independently cross-verified against a primary
news report; do that before stating as settled fact in a published piece.)

## Reach

*Surrounded by Idiots*: 55 languages, 1.5 million copies sold — DISC-style
tools remain widely used in business/coaching/organizational development
despite the validity criticism. This is not a fringe topic.
